Project 1

Squares in Grid

Theme

The composition explores how order can be created through the controlled arrangement of individual elements. Squares are positioned across a structured grid, forming distinct clusters while maintaining moments of separation and void. Repetition establishes rhythm, while changes in density, spacing, and direction introduce movement and visual focus. The resulting asymmetrical balance connects the individual elements into a cohesive composition, demonstrating how order can exist within variation.

Media

  • Swiss board
  • Black model paper

Duration

6 days

Project 2

Lines in Nature

Theme

Nature is a rich source of forms, spaces, and patterns that inspire design. This project explores the lines found in nature and how their arrangement creates order, balance, and spatial relationships. By observing and extracting natural lines, the composition investigates movement, rhythm, emphasis, variety, and harmony, transforming organic patterns into an organized visual composition of lines and spaces.

Media

  • Metal rod frame
  • Threads of 3 thicknesses
  • Black spray paint
  • Shine board
  • Swiss board
  • Photograph from nature

Duration

10 days

Project 3

Geometry in Low Relief

Theme

This low-relief composition explores depth through the layering and intersection of geometric planes. Projecting and recessed forms create a continuous dialogue between solid and void, while changes in height generate visual movement across the surface. The interaction of light and shadow emphasizes the overlapping layers, transforming a flat plane into a dynamic spatial composition.

Media

  • Styrofoam
  • Plaster of Paris

Duration

15 days

Project 4

Composition with Planes

Theme

Planes are fundamental elements in architecture that define, divide, and connect spaces. By arranging horizontal and vertical planes at varying heights, proportions, and orientations, the design creates a series of interconnected spatial zones with different degrees of enclosure. Overlapping and intersecting planes introduce hierarchy, rhythm, and directional movement, while the spaces between them maintain visual continuity. The balance between open and enclosed areas allows individual planes to work together as a unified spatial system, creating a dynamic relationship between form and space.

Media

  • White plastic wood

Duration

10 days

05

Project 5

Space and Flow by Subtracting Volumes

Theme

Space emerges through the transformation of mass by subtraction. Recessed openings and voids penetrate the layered solid, creating moments of enclosure, light, and visual connection. The variation in openings establishes rhythm and movement, while the contrast between solid mass and illuminated void creates depth and spatial continuity. Through subtraction, a simple volume is transformed into a dynamic architectural experience.

Media

  • Styrofoam

Duration

22 days

06

Project 6

Seasonscape

Fragments of Peace

Theme

Using the visual characteristics of a season as its foundation, the composition explores the relationship between form, space, and proportion. Individual elements vary in geometry, scale, orientation, and placement to establish hierarchy and rhythm. Through the interaction of overlapping and intersecting forms, a sense of balance, depth, and spatial continuity emerges, translating the qualities of the season into an abstract three-dimensional composition.

Concept

Inspired by the Kalboishakhi, the concept captures three moments of summer: the calm before the storm, the chaos within it, and the calm that follows. Through changing forms, waves, and shifting spaces, the design expresses these transitions as “Fragments of Peace.”
